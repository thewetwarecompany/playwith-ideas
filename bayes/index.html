The Test Says Yes

A thousand people. A few of them are sick. A test flags some of them, and some who are not. Hide everyone the test cleared, and look at who is left.

The ringed dots are the ones the test flags. The pale dots with a red rim are the people who have the condition. A positive result is the chance you are looking at a pale ringed dot, not merely a ringed one.
